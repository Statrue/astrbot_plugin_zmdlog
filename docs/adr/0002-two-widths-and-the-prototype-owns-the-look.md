# Only list pages keep the phone width, and the prototype owns the look

Amends ADR 0001 (2026-10-04), after the ranking prototype's variant I was
settled.

- **Two widths, not one.** The board ranking and the other ranking-style
  pages (rows of records, players or characters) stay a phone-width long
  picture, readable without zooming — ADR 0001's budget, unchanged for them.
  Every other page may be laid out wide to keep it short, and a reader is
  expected to open it and zoom on a phone. The wide width is settled by its
  first prototype.
- **The look is the one the user art-directed, not Endfield Design Skill
  V2.** Variants A–H followed the Skill and were judged ugly; variant I was
  built from screenshots of official Endfield UI, round by round, and is the
  reference. The Skill keeps only its readability floor. No rule carried over
  from the old pages — the yellow's meaning, the shared page shell, the term
  of a hero figure — binds a new page: each page family's look is decided on
  its own prototype, and a family may depart from the ranking page's (help
  may be friendlier and less rigid than a speed board).
- **The picture never says there is a next 页.** A pager, a next-page
  command line and a divider were all tried on the ranking prototype and
  rejected; page 2 is found through the help page and, on the QQ official
  bot, its buttons. The header's record count is the only hint on the image.
